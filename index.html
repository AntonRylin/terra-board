Built for your own work.

Your boards are files on your computer. Nothing goes to the cloud, to other people or to AI until you choose.

Everything in one place

Drop in images, PDFs, links and videos. Add notes, tables with owners and due dates, checklists and frames. Zoom out to see the whole project, zoom in to work.

Draw like on paper

A smooth freehand pen and a highlighter. Sketch an idea, circle what matters, mark up a screenshot or a PDF page.

Private by default

No sign-up to start, no analytics or tracking of our own, and it works offline, on a plane or a train. Privacy policy

Your AI works right on the board.

Terra has a built-in MCP server. Switch it on and Claude, Codex or any other MCP app on your computer can read your boards and build on them: notes, tables, picture cards, even live web pages.

Work on it together, live.

Turn on Terra Cloud, then invite people by email or code and edit the same board together. Changes show up on everyone's screen as they happen, with live cursors. Follow a colleague's view, put boards in a shared team space, and let viewers ask to edit.

Sharing and sync need a free Terra account. Your boards stay local until you choose to share them.

Terra for your company.

Run your own Terra sync server and keep shared boards inside your company network. IT can preset the server on every computer, or lock it.

People sign in with an email code, Google or Microsoft; single sign-on with your own identity provider is still being built.
