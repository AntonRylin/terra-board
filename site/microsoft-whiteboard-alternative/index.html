A Microsoft Whiteboard alternative that works offline on Windows

Terra Board is a free, open-source whiteboard app for Windows. It runs on your PC, works without internet, and needs no Microsoft or any other account.

What happened to Microsoft Whiteboard

According to Microsoft's support page, the standalone Microsoft Whiteboard apps for Windows, iOS and Android retired on 16 October 2026. This applies to people with work or school accounts and to people with personal Microsoft accounts.

Apps that are already installed aren't necessarily removed, but after that date they show a retirement notice instead of opening your whiteboards.

Whiteboard in Microsoft Teams and Whiteboard on the web are not part of this retirement. For work or school accounts, Microsoft says Whiteboard continues there.

Source: Microsoft Support: Retirement of the standalone Microsoft Whiteboard apps for Windows, iOS, and Android, checked on 22 October 2026. If anything here differs from that page, the page is right.

So if you used the Whiteboard app on a Windows PC, and especially if you used it with a personal account, you may be looking for a whiteboard that is an app on your PC again.

What Terra Board gives you

  • A real desktop app. Installed on Windows, opens from the Start menu, works offline.
  • No account. Nothing to sign in to. Cloud sync exists, but it's off until you turn it on.
  • Your boards are files on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Terra Board\Library. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete them.
  • Free and open source under the MIT licence. If the project ever stopped, the code would still be there.
  • An infinite canvas with sticky notes, text, shapes, frames, arrows, checklists, tables, images, video and PDFs.
  • Copy text from images and scanned PDFs with Windows' built-in text recognition, on your computer.
  • Share a board over Wi-Fi with people in the same room: they open it in their browser, and you approve each one.

Where Terra Board falls short of Whiteboard

Microsoft Whiteboard was built around drawing with a pen. Be aware of these before you switch:

  • No freehand pen yet. There's a highlighter for marking things up. A drawing pen is being built right now, but it isn't released.
  • It can't open Whiteboard files. Anything you can save as an image or PDF can be pasted or dropped onto a Terra board.
  • No Teams integration and no iPhone, iPad or Android app. Terra Board is Windows only.
  • The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns you. Click More info → Run anyway.

If you mostly typed, pasted and arranged things rather than drew, Terra Board will likely feel familiar. If you mostly drew with a pen, check back once the pen ships.
