Privacy policy

Terra Board keeps your boards on your own computer. Nothing leaves it unless you use a feature that needs the internet, such as Terra Cloud. This page explains each of those cases.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who is responsible

Terra Board is a private open-source project. The controller under the GDPR is Anton Rylin, Vienna, Austria. Email: montiesprache@gmail.com. Full details are in the Impressum.

This website

The website sets no cookies and loads no analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts or embedded content. It is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub's servers process your IP address and technical request data to deliver the page and keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub transfers data to the USA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Links to GitHub, Discord and other sites take you to those services; their own privacy policies apply there.

The app on your computer

Your boards, images and settings are stored on your computer (Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Terra Board\Library; Linux: ~/.local/share/Terra Board/Library). We have no access to them. The app contains no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.

When the app goes online

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
Update check (on by default; can be switched off in Settings → About)Shortly after start and then every 4 hours the app downloads a small version file from GitHub releases. GitHub sees your IP address. If a new version exists, the installer is downloaded from GitHub. Basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (keeping the app secure and up to date).
Extensions (only when you install one)The file is downloaded from GitHub or, for Office files, from The Document Foundation (LibreOffice). Those servers see your IP address.
Links, videos and web pages on a board (only when you add them)The app contacts the website you added to fetch a title and preview image, or to show the page. YouTube videos play through youtube-nocookie.com. Those sites see your IP address and apply their own policies.
Song links (extension, only when used)The audio is downloaded from the site you linked, for example YouTube.
Import from Miro (only when used)The app talks to Miro's API with the access token you enter. The token stays on your computer, encrypted, and only if you tick "Remember".
Share over Wi-Fi (only when used)Your computer serves the board directly to devices in your local network. Nothing passes through our servers.
MCP server for AI apps (off by default)Runs only on your computer (127.0.0.1). What your AI app does with board content is governed by that app's provider.

Terra Cloud (sync and sharing)

Terra Cloud is off until you turn it on in Settings → Cloud sync. When you turn it on, your computer gets an anonymous sign-in; there is no email or password. While sync is on, the following is stored on our server:

  • The boards, folders and objects you sync, with their images and files.
  • Your display name and, if you add them, a profile picture and an entrance song.
  • Your computer's name (so you can tell your devices apart), your role and when the device joined.
  • While others are on a shared board with you: your cursor position and current view, sent live to them and not stored.

People you share a board with can see that board, your display name, profile picture and cursor. Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because sync is the service you asked for.

The server runs on Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA) in the EU (Ireland). Supabase processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement with Standard Contractual Clauses. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

How long it is kept: until you delete it. Turning sync off removes your device from the workspace; "Remove from cloud" deletes a board's content. To have all your cloud data deleted, including files and the anonymous sign-in, email montiesprache@gmail.com and we delete it within 30 days. Expired pairing and invite codes are deleted automatically after one day.

If your company runs its own server and the app is set to use it, your company is responsible for that data, not us.

When you contact us

We use your messages and contact details only to answer you, and delete them once the matter is closed unless the law requires us to keep them (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
