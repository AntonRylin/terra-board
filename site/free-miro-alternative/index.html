A free Miro alternative that works offline

Terra Board is a free, open-source whiteboard app for Windows. Make as many boards as you like, keep them on your own PC, and use them without internet.

Why people look for one

Miro's own pricing page lists the Free plan with 3 editable boards: you can create more, but only the three most recent stay editable.

Source: miro.com/pricing, checked on 22 October 2026. Plans change; that page has the current terms.

What Terra Board does differently

Terra BoardWhat it means for you
No board limitMake as many boards as you want. Every one stays editable.
Works offlineIt's a desktop app. Your boards open and save without internet.
Files on your PCBoards live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Terra Board\Library. Export any board as a .canvas file with its media, or as PNG, JPG or PDF.
No accountNothing to sign up for. Cloud sync and sharing exist, but they're off until you turn them on.
Open source (MIT)All the code is on GitHub. You can read it, build it, or fork it.
Self-hostableA company can run its own sync server (Supabase) and point every PC at it. IT can preset or lock the server. How it works

On the board you get sticky notes, text, shapes, frames, arrows, checklists, tables with typed columns, images, video and PDFs you can read and rearrange. With sync on, you can share a board with other people and see their cursors live. Without any server, you can share a board over Wi-Fi to browsers on the same network.

When Miro is the better choice

  • You're not on Windows. Terra Board is a Windows app only, with no Mac, browser or mobile version.
  • You need templates and integrations. Terra Board has no template gallery and no connections to other work tools.
  • You need to open existing Miro boards. Terra can't import them. Images and PDFs you export can be dropped onto a board.
  • You run big workshops with many people at once. Terra's sharing is newer and simpler, and email sign-in for sync isn't there yet.
  • You draw a lot by hand. Terra has a highlighter, but no freehand pen yet. One is being built.

If you mostly plan, collect and sort things on your own or in a small team, and you'd rather own the files, Terra Board is worth a try. It's free, so trying costs you a download.
