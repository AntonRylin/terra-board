Terra Board for Android (test)

The Android app is in a closed test on Google Play. Anyone can join: it takes two clicks and a minute.

1. Join the tester group

Sign in with the Google account you use on your phone, then click Join group.

Join Terra Board testers

2. Become a tester

With the same Google account, open the testing page and click Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and reload.

Become a tester

3. Install the app

Install Terra Board from Google Play on your phone. Updates come through Google Play like any other app.

Open in Google Play

Good to know

  • Sign-in: the Android app works with Terra Cloud, so it asks you to sign in. It shows and edits the boards in your account, the same ones you sync from your computer.
  • Stay in the group while you test: leaving it removes the app's test access.
  • Feedback: tell us what breaks or what you'd like on GitHub or Discord.
  • To stop testing, click Leave the program on the testing page.
